    Spray & Weather Frequently Asked Questions
    
          
          
    
            
              What are the best hours of the day to spray?
            
            
    
              
    Early morning (7:00 AM – 10:00 AM) or late afternoon (4:00 PM – 6:30 PM). Avoid scorching midday heat (above 33°C) to prevent rapid evaporation and foliar burn.
    
            
    
          
    

          
    
            
              Why is spraying dangerous in strong winds (>15 km/h)?
            
            
    
              
    High winds blow fine chemical mist away from the target tomato plants onto nearby crops, water canals, or the applicator (chemical drift), wasting money and causing contamination.
    
            
    
          
    

          
    
            
              What if rain falls within 2 to 4 hours after spraying?
            
            
    
              
    Rain will wash off contact fungicides before they can adhere to the leaf cuticle. Most sprays require at least 2 to 4 hours of dry weather to achieve rainfastness.
    
            
    
          
    

          
    
            
              Why should I check the harvest waiting period (PHI)?
            
            
    
              
    The Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI) is the mandatory number of days between the last pesticide spray and picking tomatoes. Strictly observe PHI to ensure produce is free from chemical residues.

    CIB&RC Official Toxicity Label Codes
    

        
    
          
          
    Red Triangle: Extremely Toxic (Class I) — Highest hazard, use maximum protective gear.
    
        
    

        
    
          
          
    Yellow Triangle: Highly Toxic (Class II) — Strict precautions needed.
    
        
    

        
    
          
          
    Blue Triangle: Moderately Toxic (Class III) — Follow label safety rules.
    
        
    

        
    
          
          
    Green Triangle: Slightly Toxic (Class IV) — Safer bio-rational or organic choice.
    
        
    
      
    

      
      
    
        
    5 Golden Rules of Safe Spraying
    
        
    1. Wear protective gear: mask, gloves, and boots.
    
        
    2. Never blow blocked nozzles with your mouth; use clean water and a soft brush.
    
        
    3. Spray with the wind at your back, never walking into the spray mist.
    
        
    4. Strictly observe the harvest waiting period (PHI) before picking produce.
    
        
    5. Triple rinse empty pesticide containers and puncture them; never reuse for water or feed.
